Why files get rejected

Small file issues. Big printing headaches.

Missing bleed

Backgrounds that stop at the trim leave thin white edges once the book is cut.

Wrong spine width

Spine width depends on page count and paper. Guess it, and the title slides onto the front.

Low-resolution images

Images that look fine on screen print soft or pixelated below 300 DPI.

Fonts not embedded

Missing fonts get swapped at the printer, and your layout shifts.

Text too close to the edge

Page numbers and headers inside the cut zone can be trimmed off.

FAQ

Good questions.

Which platforms do you work with?

Amazon KDP and IngramSpark, including US and UK trim sizes. Other printers are fine too: just send their spec sheet.

What’s included in the free file check?

We check bleed, trim size, spine width, font embedding and image resolution, then send you a short summary of what needs fixing.

Do you edit or proofread my text?

No. We work on the design and print files only, so please send your final, edited manuscript.

Who owns the final files?

You do. Your content stays yours, and any stock images we use come with their license details.

How do I pay?

We accept PayPal. You’ll get a PayPal invoice with your quote, in US dollars or UK pounds.
